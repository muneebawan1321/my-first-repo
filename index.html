About Us 🐾

Founded in 2020, Paws & Polish was born from a simple belief: every pet deserves to be treated like family. What started as a small grooming studio has grown into a full-service pet care destination.

Our team of certified groomers and animal lovers bring over 15 years of combined experience. We use only premium, pet-safe products and tailor every session to your furry friend's unique needs.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend booking an appointment to guarantee your preferred time slot. Peak hours (weekends) fill up quickly!

A basic wash takes about 45-60 minutes. Full grooming (wash + haircut) typically takes 1.5-2 hours depending on breed and coat condition.

For the comfort and safety of all pets, we ask that owners drop off and pick up. However, we're happy to send you photo updates during the session!

We only use premium, pet-safe, and hypoallergenic products. All our shampoos and conditioners are free from parabens, sulfates, and artificial fragrances.
